Mandatory exit criteria (Sprint 8 → GA)
  • Sprint 8 regression gate: ≥95% pass rate across 820 regression TCs; zero regression failures in critical paths
  • Defects: zero open P1; all open P2 within 7-day aging threshold and with documented disposition
  • Compliance: PCI-DSS 4.0 and PSD2 audit evidence filed and signed off (both standards tested in S6–S7)
  • Pilot: Sprint 9 UAT sign-off from all 5 enterprise clients, with issues logged and triaged
  • Environment: non-prod stability demonstrated across two consecutive sprints (no P1/P2 outages)
  • Security: DAST + SAST clean in S7; pen-test findings closed or formally accepted
  • Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 critical-path scans run in S6–S7, no AA-level blockers
Risk-acceptance authority: Release Board (CTO + Head of Compliance + QE Lead). Recommendation flips to No-Go if any mandatory criterion is unmet at exit, or if risk R-01 (UAT instability) remains unresolved past end of Sprint 7.

01

Executive summary

For the CEO · 90-second read

We are entering Sprint 3 of 11 on the PayStream 3.0 release. Sprints 1 and 2 closed on-plan: requirements and test design complete, Payment Gateway core delivered and smoke-tested. The three-sprint rolling velocity is 37.3 points vs 39.5 planned — a 5.6% delivery shortfall. The underlying headcount gap is 10% (one open SDET role out of ten approved); the smaller delivery shortfall reflects partial coverage of the gap through overtime and reprioritisation.

Quality posture is Amber. Zero P1 defects open, seven P2 defects within the 7-day aging threshold. Fraud module shows an early rise in defect inflow — likely the complexity of the new risk-scoring rules — and is being front-loaded with exploratory testing in Sprint 3.

The single material risk is UAT environment stability. Two outages in Sprint 2 cost ~1.5 days of test execution. A dedicated test environment plus a weekly refresh is on the table and needs a decision this week to protect Sprint 8 regression.

Pilot (Sprint 9, Jan 24) and GA (Sprint 11, Feb 21) dates remain achievable with the mitigations below.

Decisions needed

  1. Approve dedicated UAT environment ($18K / quarter)
  2. Confirm SDET backfill or accept 10% velocity gap
  3. Sign off on pilot customer list (5 enterprise clients)
  4. Confirm PCI-DSS 4.0 scope by end S3 (workshop Oct 24)
  5. Approve PSD2 audit scope by end S4 (separate compliance review)
